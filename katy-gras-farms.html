The grass we carry

Two proven Houston grasses.
Cut fresh for your job.

St. Augustine

Most popular

The Houston classic. Thick, lush, and shade-tolerant — a great fit for most residential yards in this climate. Establishes fast when watered well.

  • ✦ Handles partial shade well
  • ✦ Broad, soft blade texture
  • ✦ Quick to fill in and root

Zoysia

Premium pick

Dense, fine-bladed, and remarkably resilient. A favorite for homeowners who want that manicured-lawn look with less water and stronger wear tolerance.

  • ✦ Drought & heat tolerant
  • ✦ Tight, carpet-like feel
  • ✦ Holds up to foot traffic

Not sure which is right for your yard? Call us and we'll talk you through it.
